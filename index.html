Classic Lemon Cake

A soft, zesty cake that's perfect with afternoon tea.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 3 eggs

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix dry ingredients.
  3. Fold in eggs and zest.
  4. Bake for 35 minutes.

Careful — the pan will be very hot when it comes out.
